## What users will want next (after v0.11.0)

   the dnd5e system compendium, outside the package). Needs the dnd5e system
   pack as an optional second input.
2. **Fantasy Grounds NPCs/items/story → the same 5e notes** (FG phase 3).
   Reuses PR #17's writers; FG `npc` records map well to the 5e block.
3. **Missing art**: 14 portraits and 6 tiles referenced outside the package.
   Let the user point at their Foundry `Data` folder to resolve them.
4. **Animated tiles / video** → Arkenforge animated assets (needs a sample
   capture in the Toolkit; reported as losses today).
5. **Ambient sounds placed on maps** → Arkenforge ambience presets (`.rwamb`).
6. **More systems** (Pathfinder 2e etc.): only after 5e is solid.
7. **Release-and-listen**: post v0.11.0 on the Arkenforge Discord/forum and
   collect real packages that fail.

Out of scope for now: Roll20 (licensing), AboveVTT (browser extension inside
D&D Beyond), encrypted FG Forge modules (refused by design).
